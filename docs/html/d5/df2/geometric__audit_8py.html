Detailed Description

Blocking final geometric audit (F03) and its validity record.

Status of an attempt, written to <prefix>_validity.json:

  • passed: computed minimum clearance >= requested clearance, within the declared scope (contact allowed when the requested clearance is zero);
  • failed: envelope intersection or insufficient clearance;
  • undetermined: unusable input, audit error or domain not covered.

Only passed allows the exports. Scope: a sufficient condition for the tubes of the sampled centerline (flat-ended cylinders per segment plus vertex sectors, as rasterised by export/voxelizer.py) to be disjoint, for the audit kernel validation/audit_distance.py qualified by the V1 tests. The continuous spline, self-intersections of a fiber with itself (zero shift), the separation of fibers in the voxel grid and non-circular sections are not covered. Margins are fiber-axis distances minus the sum of radii, in the length unit of the box.

Spherical pores are generated after this audit; audit_pores() then checks, as a second blocking step, that they overlap neither one another nor any fiber (periodic images included) before anything is exported.
